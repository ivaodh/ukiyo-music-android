Ukiyo Music Privacy Policy

Live in the moment

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Applies to: Ukiyo Music for Android (application ID app.ukiyo.music)

1. What Ukiyo Music is

Ukiyo Music is a private, invite-only music application used by a closed group of friends. Accounts are created by the admin. There is no public sign-up.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, account role, and authentication tokens.
  • Device information: device name, a device fingerprint derived from the Android device identifier and app package, and the last active time. This is used to enforce the device limit and prevent account sharing.
  • Library information: liked songs, playlists, recently played tracks, and playback history.
  • Usage information: app version, device model, and basic diagnostic events needed to operate the service.
  • Local downloads: audio files and metadata stored on your device for offline playback.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your session active.
  • To provide your library, playlists, history, lyrics, search, and playback.
  • To enforce the device limit and protect the service from abuse.
  • To diagnose failures and improve reliability.
  • To show maintenance notices and require app updates.

4. What we do not collect

  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Contacts, SMS, or call logs.
  • Photos, videos, or personal files.
  • Health, financial, or payment information.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising. Data is processed by our own backend, our database and authentication provider, and the infrastructure providers that host the service. Those providers process data only to operate the service.

6. Storage and security

Account and library data are stored in a hosted PostgreSQL database. Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted storage on your device. Network traffic uses HTTPS. We limit access to the backend and use short-lived signed stream URLs.

7. Retention

Account and library data are kept while your account is active. Local downloads remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. When an account is deleted, related account and library rows are removed.

8. Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from Settings.
  • Delete individual downloads or all downloads from the Downloads screen.
  • Request account and data deletion by contacting the admin.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data.

9. Children

Ukiyo Music is not directed at children. Accounts are created only for invited adults in the private group.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date at the top will be updated.
